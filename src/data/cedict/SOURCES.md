# CC-CEDICT

`cedict.gz` is the unmodified CC-CEDICT text distribution published by MDBG. CC-CEDICT is community maintained and includes work from CEDICT, copyright 1997 and 1998 Paul Andrew Denisowski.

- [Download and project attribution](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict)
- [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- [CC-CEDICT project](https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/)

The application selects exact headword entries and extracts their English glosses at runtime.
